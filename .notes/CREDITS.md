- `public/images/food/*.png` — Fluent Emoji (Microsoft), 256x256 3D PNGs for
  pizza (1f355), kebab (1f959), burger (1f354) and candy (1f36c). These are the
  icons the plot rains down and the menu lists. **MIT** licensed.
  https://github.com/microsoft/fluentui-emoji

- `public/images/ragnaduds-peco-duds.png`, `public/images/ragnaduds-orc.png` —
  sprites taken from Eduardo's own RagnaDuds site. Ragnarok Online assets are
  Gravity Co. property, used here for a non commercial personal project.

- `public/images/banana.jpg` — "Bunch of bananas on sale" by Wilfredor,
  Wikimedia Commons, released **CC0** (no attribution required, credited
  anyway). https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Bunch_of_bananas_on_sale.jpg

- `public/images/bigbang.jpg` — "CMB Timeline" by NASA / WMAP Science Team,
  Wikimedia Commons, **public domain**.
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:CMB_Timeline_No_Labels_or_WMAP.jpg

- `public/images/pepperoni-elmo.jpg` — an internet meme of unknown origin,
  supplied by Eduardo. Elmo is a Sesame Workshop character, used here as a
  joke on a personal non commercial page.
